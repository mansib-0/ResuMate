# 1. Executive Summary

ResuMate is an AI-powered career platform that transforms how job seekers create resumes, find jobs, and track applications. Starting as a SaaS product for resume optimization, our vision is to become the world's definitive career intelligence platform — the "Bloomberg Terminal for your career."

**The Problem:** Job seekers spend 3-6 hours per application customizing resumes. 75% of resumes are rejected by ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems) before a human ever sees them. The global job search market is fragmented, inefficient, and emotionally draining.

**The Solution:** ResuMate uses AI to instantly tailor resumes to match specific job descriptions, automatically discover matching jobs, and track every application on a smart Kanban board. Ultra Pro users receive real-time notifications when new matching jobs appear.

**Market Size:**
- Total Addressable Market (TAM): $47B (global HR tech)
- Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM): $12B (job search tools + resume builders)
- Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM): $500M (initial 3-year target)

**Revenue Model:** Subscription SaaS (Pro at $15/mo, Ultra Pro at $29/mo) expanding into enterprise B2B, recruitment marketplace, and career coaching.

**Target Valuation Timeline:**
- Year 1: $5M (Pre-Seed/Seed)
- Year 2: $50M (Series A)
- Year 3: $250M (Series B)
- Year 4: $750M (Series C)
- Year 5: $1B+ (Series D / Pre-IPO)

## 3.3 Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Weakness | ResuMate Advantage |
|---|---|---|
| Canva Resume | No AI tailoring, no ATS optimization | AI keyword injection per job |
| Resumé.io | Template-only, no job matching | Auto job search from CV |
| Jobscan | Expensive ($50/mo), no resume editing | All-in-one: edit + match + track |
| LinkedIn Easy Apply | Generic resume for all jobs | Unique resume per application |

1. **Data Moat:** Every resume processed teaches our AI what keywords get interviews → proprietary dataset.
2. **Network Effects:** As more users apply through ResuMate, we learn which resume formats get callbacks, creating an unfair advantage.
3. **Switching Costs:** Application history, saved resumes, and job tracker data lock users in.
4. **Brand:** "ResuMate" becomes a verb — "I ResuMated my resume."

# 6. Legal & Corporate Structure

## 6.1 Company Registration (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Register in Bangladesh (Immediate)
1. **Sole Proprietorship** via RJSC (Registrar of Joint Stock Companies and Firms)
   - Cost: ~3,000 BDT
   - Time: 1-2 weeks
   - Required: NID, TIN certificate, trade license
2. Get a **Trade License** from Dhaka City Corporation
   - Cost: ~5,000-10,000 BDT
3. Register for **TIN (Tax Identification Number)** at NBR
4. Open a **business bank account** at a local bank (DBBL, Brac Bank, or City Bank recommended for tech startups)

### Step 2: Register in the USA (Before Fundraising)
1. **Incorporate a Delaware C-Corporation** via Stripe Atlas ($500)
   - Why Delaware: 90% of US startups + all VCs require it
   - Stripe Atlas provides: Incorporation + EIN + Mercury bank account + Carta cap table + legal templates
2. **Register as a Foreign Corporation** in your operating state (if needed)
3. Get an **EIN (Employer Identification Number)** from IRS (free)
4. Open a **Mercury** or **Brex** business bank account (both are startup-friendly, online, no minimum balance)

### Step 3: Holding Structure
```
ResuMate Inc. (Delaware C-Corp) — Parent Company
  ├── ResuMate BD Ltd. (Bangladesh) — R&D & Engineering
  ├── ResuMate EU Ltd. (Ireland/Estonia) — EU Operations (Year 3+)
```

- Keep receipts for everything (crucial for tax deductions)

## 7.2 Tax Obligations

### USA (Delaware C-Corp)
- **Federal Corporate Tax:** 21% on profits
- **Delaware Franchise Tax:** $400/year minimum
- **State Tax:** 0% in Delaware (no corporate income tax on out-of-state revenue)
- **Quarterly Estimated Tax:** File Form 1120-ES
- **Annual Filing:** Form 1120 (due March 15 or with extension September 15)
- **Sales Tax:** Varies by state where customers are (use Stripe Tax to auto-calculate)

### Bangladesh
- **Personal Income Tax:** 0-30% graduated
- **Corporate Tax (if BD entity):** 20% for IT-enabled services (reduced rate!)
- **VAT:** 15% standard, but IT/ITES services may qualify for VAT exemption
- **File annually** with NBR (National Board of Revenue)

### Key Tax Strategies
1. **R&D Tax Credit** — The US offers up to $250K/year in R&D tax credits for startups. Your AI development qualifies!
2. **Transfer Pricing** — Pay the BD subsidiary a fair market rate for engineering services. This moves some profit to the lower-tax BD entity legally.
3. **QSBS Exclusion** — If the Delaware C-Corp qualifies as a Qualified Small Business Stock (QSBS), founders can exclude up to $10M in capital gains tax when they sell shares!
